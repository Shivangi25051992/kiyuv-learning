Privacy Policy

Kiyuv Learning Academy · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Draft for legal review. This text describes what the app does today. A children's-privacy lawyer must review it before the app is released to the public. Items in [square brackets] are still to be filled in.

Kiyuv Learning Academy ("the app") is a learning app for children aged 2 to 13. It is made by [company legal name] ("we"). Parents create the account and the children's profiles. Children do not sign up or enter their own details. Contact: [privacy contact email].

What we collect, and why

InformationWhy
Parent: email address and sign-in details from Google or Apple (or email and password)To sign the parent in and keep the family's profiles safe.
Parent PIN (stored only as a one-way hash)To keep the Parent area and profile changes away from children.
Child profile: nickname, age in years, country, optional state or region, favourite colour, avatarTo show age-appropriate lessons and to personalise the app. We never ask for a birth date, address, photo or school.
Child's first name (optional). It is stored encrypted and shown only to the parent.So the parent can recognise the profile.
Learning progress: stars, streaks, badges and results per topicTo save progress and show it to the child and parent.
Family groups (optional, off by default): a name the parent chooses for the child, an avatar and weekly starsTo show a weekly leaderboard to the families in the same group. The board never shows a first name, nickname, age, country, state or email.
Consent recordsTo keep a record of what the parent agreed to.
On the device only: saved rounds, daily feelings check-in, outfits, settingsTo make the app work. These never leave the device.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no tracking of children across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or marketing tools in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • Children cannot chat, post, or contact anyone through the app.

Where it is kept, and who helps us

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Hosting) in the Google Cloud region in Mumbai, India (asia-south1). Google processes it for us under its own terms. Sign-in with Google or Apple is run by those companies. Sounds and pictures are bundled in the app. The device's own text-to-speech reads words aloud.

Your choices

  • See or download your data: Parent area → Your data → Download my data.
  • Delete everything: Parent area → Your data → Delete my account. This permanently deletes the account, all child profiles and progress, and removes the family from any groups.
  • Edit or reset a child's profile: Parent area, with the parent PIN.
  • You can also write to [privacy contact email] with any request.

Keeping information safe

Connections are encrypted. Children's first names are encrypted, PINs are hashed, and the database only lets a signed-in parent with a recent PIN read their own family's data.

How long we keep it

We keep a family's data until the parent deletes the account. [Retention for inactive accounts to be confirmed.]

Children's privacy

The app is meant for children, and parents are in control of every child's profile. We collect only what the app needs, and we do not knowingly collect information from a child without the parent's involvement.
